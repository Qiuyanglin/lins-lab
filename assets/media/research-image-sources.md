These images illustrate research areas; the cards link to example papers, not an exhaustive or ranked selection. Cards are ordered by publication date, newest first.

- `research-nanopore-application-no-labels.png`: a label-free conceptual adaptation of the author-supplied journal manuscript's Figure 2(a), made with the built-in imagegen tool at the author's request. It illustrates DNA translocation and ionic-current readout; it is not a device photograph or measured result. The publication link remains the published VLSI 2026 conference paper. The unchanged extracted source is `research-nanopore-principle.png`; the editing prompt is recorded in `research-nanopore-application.prompt.txt`.
