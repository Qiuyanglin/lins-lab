- `research-ppg-applications.jpg`: Figure 1 of the TBCAS 2021 expanded paper, DOI `10.1109/TBCAS.2021.3130470`, extracted unchanged from the author's local PDF. The homepage displays the wearable-device application panel. It is contextual PPG imagery, not a photograph of the ISSCC chest-recording prototype.
- `research-bioreactor-overview.jpg`: Figure 21.2.1, ISSCC 2023, DOI `10.1109/ISSCC42615.2023.10067298`, extracted unchanged from the author's local PDF. The homepage displays the bioreactor application panel.
- `selected-neural-cicc2026.png`: the existing `content/post/CICC26/Invasive.png`, showing the brain/electrode portion of the CICC 2026 review's Figure 1, DOI `10.1109/CICC65509.2026.11509514`.

Source images remain intact. Hugo performs thumbnail cropping and WebP conversion; no measured data or hardware details are synthesized.
