---
title: Lab Welcomes Four New Students
date: 2026-09-14
---

The laboratory is pleased to welcome four new members: **Ding Xu**, **Jiajie Lao**, **Zining Liu**, and **Junyu Tang**. Based on their outstanding academic performance, all four students have received recommendation-based admission and will join Prof. Qiuyang Lin’s research group at the Institute of Frontiers Technology for Chips and Systems, Fudan University, in 2027. Ding Xu and Jiajie Lao will pursue Ph.D. degrees, while Zining Liu and Junyu Tang will pursue master’s degrees.

<!--more-->

The four students come from South China University of Technology, Nanjing University, Xiamen University, and Northwestern Polytechnical University, respectively. During their undergraduate studies, they have maintained excellent academic records and gained research and engineering experience in analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits, analog front ends, power management, data converters, emerging memory devices, and digital hardware design.

The students’ previous research experience spans the following areas:

- Analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits
- Biomedical integrated circuits and analog front ends for biosignal acquisition
- Analog-to-digital converters and low-power operational amplifiers
- LDOs and power management integrated circuits
- Emerging memory devices, computing-in-memory, and current-readout circuits
- Digital hardware design and integrated circuit verification

All four students earned recommendation-based admission through their strong academic records and overall performance, reflecting the laboratory’s emphasis on solid technical foundations, research potential, and practical engineering experience when selecting new members. The laboratory will continue to investigate high-performance and highly integrated chips and systems for biomedical and intelligent sensing applications.

We warmly welcome Ding Xu, Jiajie Lao, Zining Liu, and Junyu Tang to the laboratory and look forward to their future growth, collaboration, and research contributions!
