We are pleased to welcome **Siyi Yuan** as a new member of our lab. She will join Prof. Qiuyang Lin’s research group at the Institute for Frontier Technologies of Integrated Circuits and Systems, Fudan University, as a **special-program master’s student jointly trained by Fudan University and Hua Hong**. Her research will focus on biomedical electronics and analog front-end integrated circuit design.

<!--more-->



  <p style="text-align:left;">
    Siyi Yuan received her B.Eng. degree from the School of Integrated Circuits at Xidian University in <strong>2026</strong>. During her undergraduate studies, she ranked among the top students in her cohort and developed a solid foundation in integrated circuit design. She received the <strong>National Scholarship</strong> and was recognized as an undergraduate <strong>“Youth Role Model.”</strong> She also gained practical experience through projects involving amplifiers and programmable gain amplifiers (PGAs).
  </p>

  <p style="text-align:left;">
    In 2026, Siyi joined Prof. Qiuyang Lin’s research group at the Institute for Frontier Technologies of Integrated Circuits and Systems, Fudan University, to pursue her master’s degree. As a <strong>special-program master’s student jointly trained by Fudan University and Hua Hong</strong>, her research interests include biomedical electronics, analog front-end integrated circuits, amplifiers, and programmable gain amplifiers.
  </p>

  <p style="text-align:left;">
    Outside her studies and research, she enjoys hiking, swimming, and playing badminton. She also enjoys reading literature and books on cognition and ways of thinking.
  </p>

</div>

Siyi’s arrival will further strengthen the lab’s research in biomedical electronics and analog front-end integrated circuits. Through a specialized project conducted with Hua Hong, she will explore related integrated circuit technologies and continue developing her technical expertise through hands-on project experience.

Welcome to the lab, Siyi! We look forward to seeing her explore new ideas and make steady progress in her future studies and research.
