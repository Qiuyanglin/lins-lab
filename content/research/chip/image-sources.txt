Source: the author-supplied Chip Gallery.vsdx, page 8 named "Chip Gallery".
Only that page's 21 embedded images were extracted. Other pages were not used.
The Visio source is unchanged. The existing chip.png collage is also unchanged
and remains available through the overview link.

data/chip_gallery.json maps every card to its original embedded media filename.
20 BMP files were converted losslessly to PNG with Windows System.Drawing;
decoded RGBA pixels and dimensions were verified identical to the source BMPs.
image6.emf was rendered with System.Drawing to a 2000 x 1292 white-backed PNG.
All images retain their original composition and scientific annotations. Visio
shape data confirmed no cropping, rotation or flipping was applied to these items.

Hugo generates responsive WebP previews without enlarging the original PNGs.
Full-size links open the matching PNG. Titles, publication years and links are
provided separately in bilingual HTML.

Publication links and displayed issue years follow the verified local publication
catalogue rather than stale collage labels. The non-contact ECG journal is TBCAS
2019 (its conference version was VLSI 2018); the JSSC amplifier, JSSC VCO ADC and
OJCAS TFT amplifier have final issue year 2026. The latter image was matched to
Figure 9 of IEEE document 11304256. The current-mode PPG journal is JSSC 2024.
