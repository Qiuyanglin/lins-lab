Research integrity is part of everyday research practice. The guidelines below cover supervision, authorship and responsible conduct.

- Identify and evaluate risks throughout a research project.
- Review methods and check whether the data support the conclusions.
- Develop research skills through supervision, practice and collaboration.
- Welcome peer review and open scholarly discussion.
