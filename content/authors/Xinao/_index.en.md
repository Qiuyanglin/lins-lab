**Xinao Ji** received his B.Eng. degree in Microelectronics Science and Engineering from Xi’an Jiaotong University (XJTU), China, in 2023, where he graduated with excellent academic performance and ranked among the top students in his major. He was then admitted to the M.Sc. program in Electronic Science and Technology at Xi’an Jiaotong University through a recommended graduate admission program. During his master’s study, he focused on analog and mixed-signal circuit design and participated in several research projects and academic competitions related to integrated circuit design. He received his M.Sc. degree in 2026.

He is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree in Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering at Fudan University (FDU) under the supervision of Prof. Qiuyang Lin.

His research interests include analog and mixed-signal circuits, current conversion circuits, analog-to-digital converters, analog front-end circuits, and integrated circuits for biomedical and wearable applications.
