Zexi Fu is a **third-year undergraduate in the Integrated Circuit Leadership Program at Fudan University's School of Integrated Circuits and Micro-Nano Electronics Innovation**, and is currently a research intern in the lab. He received **Second Prize in the Shanghai undergraduate division of the 2025 China Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling**, and **Third Prize in the national finals of the FPGA Zhongke Yihai Micro Track at the 10th National College Students Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition**.

He is particularly interested in **neural interfaces, neural electrode fabrication and brain–computer interface electronics**. By exploring the connections between electrodes, devices and circuits, he hopes to develop his understanding of intelligent electronics for biomedical and healthcare applications. During his research internship, he aims to deepen his knowledge of circuit principles and system design while gaining hands-on experimental and engineering experience. His long-term ambition is to become a leader in integrated circuits and develop practical brain–computer interfaces that address real medical and healthcare needs.

Outside my studies, I enjoy football, gaming and travel. I also love watching team competitions of all kinds—cheering together and sharing the disappointments.
