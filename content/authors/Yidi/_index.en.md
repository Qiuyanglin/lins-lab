**Yidi Sun** is currently an undergraduate student majoring in Electronic Science and Technology at Hunan University. She has been recommended for admission to the Master’s program in Electronic Information at Fudan University. Her research interests focus on brain–computer interface circuits. She has received an international scholarship and won the Second Prize in the National Finals of the China Undergraduate Integrated Circuit Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition.

In her daily life, she enjoys traveling, hiking, and swimming. She hopes that one day she can design a practical brain–computer interface circuit that can be applied in real-world scenarios, enabling more people to perceive the world through technology—while continuing to explore mountains, rivers, and oceans with curiosity about the unknown.
