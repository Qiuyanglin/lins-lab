## Research Areas

## Requirements

- Background in Microelectronics, Electrical Engineering, Integrated Circuits, Biomedical Engineering, or related fields
- Solid foundation in analog or mixed-signal integrated circuit design
- Strong interest in biomedical electronics, neural interfaces, or low-power IC design
- Good English reading and writing skills for academic research
- Experience with MATLAB / Python / Verilog / Cadence is preferred
- Experience in chip design, research projects, or publications is a plus
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a research team

## Application Materials

- Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- Academic transcripts and English test scores
- Description of research or project experience
- Publications or technical reports (if available)
