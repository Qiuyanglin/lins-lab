## Internship Activities {#activities}

We welcome undergraduate students with an interest in research to join the laboratory for practical research experience. Under the supervision of graduate students or faculty members, interns will participate in the design of basic circuit blocks and experimental research, gradually becoming familiar with integrated circuit design and the research process.

Internship activities may include:

- Design and analysis of amplifier circuits, such as operational amplifiers and low-noise amplifiers
- Analog filter and signal-conditioning circuit design
- Basic architectures and design methods for analog-to-digital converters (ADCs) and digital-to-analog converters (DACs)
- Design of basic circuit blocks for biosignal acquisition
- Circuit modeling, simulation, and system-level verification
- Analog and mixed-signal circuit design workflows and the use of EDA tools
- PCB-level circuit design and debugging
- Fundamentals of analog IC layout design

## What You Will Gain {#learning}

Through the internship, you will have opportunities to:

- Develop **fundamental circuit design and engineering implementation skills**
- Learn **circuit modeling, simulation, and debugging methods**
- Understand **IC design workflows and layout fundamentals**
- Gain experience with **PCB-level circuit design and experimental verification**
- Learn **basic research methods and ways of approaching research questions**
- Participate in laboratory technical discussions and research projects

Outstanding interns may have opportunities to continue conducting research or pursue graduate study in the laboratory.

## Requirements {#requirements}

- Be an undergraduate student in Electrical Engineering, Microelectronics, Integrated Circuits, Biomedical Engineering, or a related field
- Have foundational knowledge of analog or digital circuits
- Be interested in integrated circuit design and research
- Have good learning skills and work well as part of a team

Experience with the following tools is preferred:

- MATLAB / Python
- Verilog / SystemVerilog
- Cadence or LTspice circuit simulation
- PCB design tools, such as Altium or KiCad
