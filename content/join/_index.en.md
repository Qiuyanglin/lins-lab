        Our research focuses on **Biomedical Integrated Circuits (Biomedical ICs)** and **Neural Interface Technologies**, with an emphasis on low-power analog and mixed-signal circuits for wearable and implantable biomedical systems.

        We are continuously recruiting **Postdoctoral Researchers, PhD Students, Master Students, and Undergraduate Research Interns** to join our group. Students with backgrounds in Electrical Engineering, Microelectronics, Integrated Circuits, or Biomedical Engineering are encouraged to apply.

        For undergraduate interns, we aim to provide early exposure to scientific research. Students will have opportunities to develop fundamental engineering skills, learn basic research methodologies, and gain a deeper understanding of integrated circuit design and biomedical electronics.

        ### Research Areas

        - **Biomedical Integrated Circuits for Wearable and Implantable Systems**

        - **Neural Interface Circuits for Brain–Machine Interfaces**

        - **Analog Front-End and Data Converters for Biosignal Acquisition**

        - **Bio ICs for Emerging Biomedical Applications**

        If you are interested in these research topics, please feel free to contact us.
