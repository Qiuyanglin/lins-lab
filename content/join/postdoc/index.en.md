## Requirements {#requirements}

- Hold or be about to receive a PhD in Microelectronics, Electrical Engineering, Integrated Circuits, Biomedical Engineering, or a related field
- Have experience in analog or mixed-signal integrated circuit design
- Have good English proficiency and the ability to write academic papers independently
- Experience with chip tape-out, system validation, or research projects is preferred
- A record of high-quality publications in related fields is preferred
- Be able to conduct research independently
- Work well as part of a team
